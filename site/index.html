Your agents. In your pocket.

Cornercase remote follows, answers and starts the coding agents that cornercase runs on your computer, from your phone.

Coming soon on Google Play

Android: download the APK from GitHub It installs outside Google Play, until the app is there.

Free. No account, no sign-in. It needs cornercase on your computer.

See who needs you.

Every agent of your projects on one board, with its status and for how long it has been in it. The ones that need you come first, and the agents they started fold under them. The board updates live.

Answer an agent.

Tap an agent to read its conversation and its last message, see what it is doing right now, and answer it. When it asks a question, its options show as buttons, and a row of keys sends what a prompt needs.

EnterTab↑↓EscCtrl-C

Open the live terminal.

See the agent's terminal as it runs, fitted to your phone's width, and type into it with the keys a terminal needs.

Check the changes.

See which files a workspace changed, read each diff and mark it viewed, as cornercase's changes panel does.

Start new work.

Start an agent with a prompt, in a workspace that is already open or in a new worktree, with the agent, account and model you pick. Or start an agent or a flow on an issue from any tracker cornercase is connected to.

Browse the files.

Browse the workspace's files, read a file or a picture, and search them by name.

What it needs.

The agents keep running in cornercase on your computer. The app follows them from your phone.

cornercase on your computer

cornercase 0.13.78 or later, running on your computer (Linux or macOS).

Install cornercase

A phone that can reach it

On the same network as the computer, or with both on your Tailscale network.

No account

No account and no sign-in. The app collects nothing and talks only to the machines you pair it with.

Privacy policy

Pair it with your computer

In cornercase on your computer, open Settings › Remote › Phone app, then scan its QR code from the app's Machines screen, or paste the code.

Try it without a computer

Tap Try a demo on the first screen to open a demo machine simulated on the phone, marked DEMO, with sample agents to read, message, answer, start and close. It uses no network and saves nothing.
