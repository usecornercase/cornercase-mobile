# Look

Follows cornercase-gpui's `design/widgets.md` and `src/theme.rs`: usecornercase.dev's colours, glass on one ground, rounded shapes with air, cobalt, the mark, motion. The tokens are ported from `theme.rs` into CSS custom properties; what changes for touch and small screens is recorded here. gpui's sources were read on `main` on 2026-11-08.

Topics, in `design/look/`:

- [Launch and icon](look/launch.md) (`index.html`'s launch layer, `src/lib/launch.ts`, `src-tauri/gen/apple/` for the launch, `src-tauri/icons/`): no white flash, one launch layer in the page until the app knows what to show and its cap, the storyboard's plain ground, the mark's place, Android's splash, the app icon drawn from two SVGs, its ground, regenerating it, what was left out and checked live.
- [Wide windows](look/wide.md) (`src/lib/layout.svelte.ts`, `src/lib/pointer.ts`, `src/lib/ui/popover.ts`, `src/App.svelte`, `src/lib/theme/`): the iPad and tablets, one pane or two by the window's width, `html.split` and the pane's tokens, the side and the detail, what the side opens replacing the detail, no back over the board and no edge swipe, the column and form widths with the bar across the pane, the terminal's `Full width`, a hardware keyboard, the pointer's hover, the close confirmation as a popover, the limits.
- [Navigation](look/navigation.md) (`src/lib/router.svelte.ts`, `src/lib/navigation.ts`, `src/lib/platform.ts`, `src/lib/backSwipe.ts`, `src/lib/edge.svelte.ts`, `src/lib/backButton.ts`): the router as state and its base, the ways in and back, the back button and its label, one `goBack`, the iOS edge swipe (thresholds, touch events, the shared edge, `translateX`, lifting), Android's variant and its system back, what was checked live and what was rejected.

## Tokens

- **`src/lib/theme/tokens.css` keeps `theme.rs`'s names in kebab-case** (`brand_light` is `--brand-light`, `radius::CARD` is `--radius-card`), so a colour is found by the same name in both repositories. The colours are `mod colours`; `--glass` is gpui's opaque ground, the one it uses on Linux.
- **The whole palette is ported, not only what the first screen uses.** The states, `--veil`, `--field`, `--selection` and the rest are one piece of data copied as a whole, so the two repositories cannot drift apart; that is not dead code, which is about functions and components. A test checks that every `var(--x)` in `src/` is defined, in `tokens.css` or in the same file (a component's own property, like `Mark`'s `--unit`); it does not check that every token is used.
- **Spacing, fonts and durations are not in `theme.rs`, so their names are ours.** `--gap` (8 px) and the panel margins `--inset` (12 px) and `--inset-wide` (16 px) come from the literals in gpui's `ui/mod.rs`; `--inset-card` (28 px) is a card's own padding, shared by `Emblem`'s page and the actions under it so that the two keep the card symmetric; `--enter`, `--leave` and `--pop` from `ui/motion.rs`'s `ENTER`, `LEAVE` and `POP`. The diff's tints are ours too, since `theme.rs` has none: `--diff-added` and `--diff-removed` are `--done` and `--failed` at 10 %, their `-strong` forms, for the words that changed, at 32 % (`design/changes-and-files.md` › The screens). The durations also live in `src/lib/ui/motion.ts`, which the transitions read, and a test keeps the two equal.
- **No Tokyo Night colour** in the app's own look: Tokyo Night is the terminal's palette only, kept with the terminal (`src/lib/terminal/style.ts`, Terminal › The grid › Colours and styles).
- **Contrast: `--faint` is for what is not read** (review of 2026-11-15). On `--glass` it is 4.0:1, and 3.4:1 on `--raised`, under AA's 4.5:1 for text, so text that carries information (the board's times, the thread's times and `More to come…`, the live line's time, the changes' and files' tags) and the fields' placeholders are `--muted`, 6.2:1. `--faint` stays, as in gpui, for the idle ring, disabled icons, the files' chevrons, a viewed file's dimmed counts and the diff's line numbers, a gutter read only beside its line. The token itself is gpui's and did not change. **The primary button's gradient runs from `--brand` to `--brand-hover`** (review of 2026-11-15), not gpui's `--brand-light` to `--brand`: `--on-brand` on `--brand-light` was about 3.7:1 at its top, under AA for a 16 px label, and is now 4.8:1 at its lightest. It is one step darker than gpui's, the same cobalt; the edge in `--brand-light` and the glow stay. Pressed it is `--brand-hover` whole, with the 0.97 scale. Rejected: keeping gpui's gradient as an accepted exception, for the app's most used button.
- Colours with an alpha are written `rgb(r g b / a)` from the token's channels, not `color-mix()`, which iOS 15 lacks (the deployment target is 15.0).

## Ground and glass

- **The ground is opaque.** In gpui the glass shows the desktop behind the window; behind the WebView there is nothing, so the page paints gpui's Linux ground, `--glass`, with `glass::wash` at the top: `--brand` at 12 % fading to nothing over the top 180 px. The wash is on a `position: fixed` layer, so it does not scroll away and the rubber band of iOS scrolling never shows a light gap.
- **Floating cards keep gpui's values**: the vertical gradient from `#1c1f24` at 88 % to `#101215` at 91 %, the 1 px border, the `lift` shadow and the highlight along the top. Unlike gpui, WebKit does blur the page's own content under a `backdrop-filter`, so `blur(24px)` is set with and without the `-webkit-` prefix (iOS 15–17 only read the prefixed one). On the empty screen only the ground and the wash sit behind the card, so the blur barely shows; it will matter once content scrolls under a card. Blur costs GPU on large scrolling surfaces, so it goes on floating cards only, never on lists or the ground.
- **Checked by eye in the iPhone 17 simulator, iOS 27.2, on 2026-11-08**: the card stands out from the ground at gpui's alphas, so none changed. The card's title is 20 px semibold, its lines 16 px with a line height of 1.4, the wordmark 22 px and the button's label 16 px medium. gpui sets 12–13 px text and a 17 px title for a mouse and a desktop; on a phone, body text goes to 15–17 px and titles to 20–22 px. The lines use `text-wrap: pretty`, which kept a single word from ending a paragraph; older WebKit ignores it. The mark and the card sit at the top of the screen rather than its middle, as the screens with machines will.

## Touch and motion

- **Every control is at least 44 px tall**, Apple's minimum touch target, and the pressed state is `:active`. Hover shows only while a mouse, trackpad or pen is in use (`html.pointing`, 062, [Wide windows](look/wide.md) › Pointer), never after a finger's tap.
- **WebKit on iOS applies `:active` only to elements with a touch listener**, so `Button` and each toast carry an empty `ontouchstart`. Checked with a real touch on 2026-11-09 in the iPhone 17 simulator, iOS 27.2 (an XCUITest press, which goes through the simulator's touch events like a finger): while `Add machine` is held it is `--brand-hover` (`#0050d8` read in the screenshots), and it goes back on release. Held for 10 s it does not click, since WebKit takes it as a long press; a tap opens the form.
- **Four tones, `primary`, `plain`, `danger` and `link`** (link, below): primary is cobalt, plain is gpui's `filled` of `Tone::Plain` (a `--hover` fill, a `--border` edge and `--soft` text), pressed to `--selected` with an `--edge` border. A form pairs them: `Cancel` plain, the action primary. Danger came with its first use, deleting a machine (027): `--failed` text on `--failed` at 10 % with an edge at 35 %, pressed to 18 % and 70 % (`color-mix`, so it follows the token). It marks only what cannot be undone: `Delete machine` and its confirmation's `Delete`, the chat's `Ctrl-C` once armed, whose next tap interrupts the agent (Chat › Keys), and closing an agent (Board › Closing an agent).
- **A quiet button** (`quiet`, review of 2026-11-15) keeps its tone's text and drops its fill and edge, its padding pulled out to the left so its label lines up with the text above it, still 44 px tall: the chat's `Close agent`, danger in words only, so it no longer outweighs the conversation under the title while it still says what it cannot undo. Pressed, it takes its tone's pressed fill without an edge. Rejected: plain, which would hide that it cannot be undone; a `⋯` menu (Board › Closing an agent).
- **A link** (`tone="link"`, 068) is a button that reads as a word in a sentence: `--accent` text, no fill or edge, the size of the text around it, 6 px of padding on each side, which stands for the space before it, and still 44 px tall; pressed it goes to 70 % opacity, and the keyboard's focus ring is the one every button has. The first is `Try a demo` in `DemoOffer`. Rejected: an `a` with no `href`, which is not focusable and is not announced as a button; a separate `TextLink`, which would copy the press, the touch listener and the 44 px of `Button`.
- **A resting button** keeps the primary fill at 40 % opacity and is `disabled`, `aria-disabled` and deaf to touches, like gpui's `resting_button`.
- **A busy button** is gpui's `busy_button`: the spinner before a label that says what is going on (`Adding…`), `disabled`, `aria-busy` and deaf to touches, but at full opacity, since it is working and not unavailable. With Reduce Motion the spinner stands still and the label alone says that it works.
- **The spinner** (`Spinner.svelte`) is Lucide's `loader-circle` at 12 px turning once every 900 ms in CSS, gpui's `spin`. It is mounted only while something works, so it never turns at rest.
- **Motion follows `ui/motion.rs`**: entering rises 14 px and fades in over 180 ms with `cubicOut` (gpui's `ease_out`), leaving fades out over 120 ms with `quadIn` (its `ease_in`), both from `svelte/transition` and `svelte/easing`. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, every duration is 0. Checked on 2026-11-08 in the same simulator with Reduce Motion on and off, on a recording of the launch read frame by frame: with motion, the card passes through intermediate positions and brightness; with Reduce Motion, it appears in its final place in one frame. Nothing animates forever while idle.
- **`dropIn` enters from the top**: `riseIn` with the 14 px negated, for the toasts, which come down from the top edge; they leave with `fadeOut`.
- **The pulse is gpui's `motion::pulse`**: opacity from 1 to 0.3 and back over 1,400 ms (`--pulse`, which a test keeps equal to `PULSE_MS`), on the connection bar's dot and the cursor of the mark on `home`, only while connecting (Connection › The connection bar). It is a CSS animation, not a Svelte transition, since it loops, defined once in `base.css` as the global `.pulsing` class, which each component sets; with Reduce Motion it does not run. It and the working orbit (Components) are the only animations that last, and each ends with its state.
- **A chevron on a row's right edge means going to another screen**, as in iOS's lists, so what unfolds in place keeps it off that edge: the files' tree puts its chevron on the left, and the board the same chevron below the row's dot, halfway toward the title (051, 054, a triangle until the review of 2026-11-15, Board › Tree and folding), both Lucide's `chevron-right` at 16 px in `--soft`, since the user took its old arrow on the right for the way to the chat. The chat's subagents line is the one left on the right (Board › Left out).
- **Buttons give under the finger** (review of 2026-11-15): `Button` scales to 0.97 on `:active` over `--pop` (110 ms) with a strong ease-out (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`), as well as taking its pressed colour, so a tap reads as heard even where the colour step is small (`plain` on the ground). Only `transform` moves; with Reduce Motion it does not scale and keeps the colour. Rows, icon buttons and keys keep the colour alone: they are tapped many times a minute, and a whole row shrinking reads as a jolt. Rejected until 062: hover styles, which touch screens lack; since, they show only with a pointer (Wide windows › Pointer), with no scale.
- **The sheet comes from the bottom edge** (review of 2026-11-15): `Sheet` slides up its whole height (`y: 100%`, opaque all the way) over `SHEET_IN_MS` (280 ms, `quintOut`) while its veil fades in, and on leaving slides down over `SHEET_OUT_MS` (200 ms, `cubicOut`, fast at first) while the veil fades out, so it leaves the way it came and faster than it came, as iOS's sheets. It rose 14 px and faded like a card until then, and the veil popped in at once. 280 ms is above the 180 ms of `ENTER_MS`, which is for things that appear in place: a sheet travels about 170 pt. The durations live only in `motion.ts`, since no CSS reads them. Sampled in Chromium on 2026-11-15: the sheet's top went 844, 765, 704, 686, 677, 675 pt at 40 ms steps. With Reduce Motion it appears and goes at once, as every transition here.
- **The switch slides**: `Switch`'s knob moves over `--enter` with the same ease-out, its track's colour with `ease`; at once with Reduce Motion. It jumped until the review of 2026-11-15, unlike iOS's own.
- **A keyboard's focus shows**: a hardware keyboard (an iPad, a Bluetooth one) gets a 2 px `--accent` outline 2 px off buttons, links, a `details`' summary and checkboxes on `:focus-visible`, set once in `base.css` under `:where()` so a component can still draw its own; fields keep their border and ring (Forms). WebKit's thin default ring hardly showed on the dark ground.
- **The board's chevron turns**: it rotates 90° over `ENTER_MS` as the branch unfolds and back as it folds, with a CSS transition's default `ease`, at once with Reduce Motion (a `--turn` of 0 ms, read once from `prefersReducedMotion`).
- **`.spoken` hides text from the eye and not from VoiceOver**, such as the connection bar's `Retrying` without its countdown (and the board's `h1` until the review of 2026-11-15, Board › Start): one global class in `base.css`, like `.pulsing`, rather than a copy in each component's style.

## Fonts

- **System fonts only**: `--font-ui` is `system-ui` (SF Pro on iOS) and `--font-mono` is `ui-monospace` (SF Mono). The website's Archivo and JetBrains Mono were rejected for now: they add weight to the bundle, bring their licences into the app, and the CSP allows only `'self'`, so they would have to be bundled rather than fetched.

## Icons

- **Lucide is copied, not installed**, as gpui does in `assets/icons/`: version 0.460.0, with its ISC `LICENSE` next to the shapes in `src/lib/ui/icons/`. A package would bring in thousands of icons for the few the app draws.
- **The SVG's shapes are a table in `icons/shapes.ts`**, drawn by `Icon.svelte` with `stroke="currentColor"` and a stroke width of 1.75. Rejected: the `.svg` file pasted into a component (its `<!-- @license -->` line is a markup comment, which `scripts/check-rules.mjs` refuses), imported raw into `{@html}` (an error under `eslint-plugin-svelte`'s recommended rules), an `<img>` (it does not inherit `currentColor`) and a CSS `-webkit-mask-image` (it depends on the prefix on iOS 15 and goes through the CSP's `img-src`). An icon is added to the table, with its shapes copied from the same Lucide version, when a screen first needs it.

## Toasts

- **Where they come from**: the `toast` event, and the UI's own failures: a command's `CommandError` and a rejected IPC promise, which the bindings throw rather than return (`Toasts.failed`). The UI's own `info` toasts say what an action did where no screen shows it: a started agent's `warning`, and a workspace created (`design/starting-work.md`).
- **The logic is pure** (`src/lib/toasts.ts`, with the clock passed in), and `toasts.svelte.ts` holds the stack in `$state` with one timer set for the next toast to expire, rather than one per toast.
- **Adapted from gpui's `app/toasts.rs`**:
  - At most 3 rather than 5, the oldest dropped when a fourth comes: a phone has room for fewer.
  - At the top, under the connection bar and inside the safe area, rather than at the bottom right. On a phone the bottom belongs to the keyboard and the home indicator, and iOS's own banners come from the top. They enter downward (`dropIn`).
  - Each lasts 4 s (`info`) or 8 s (`error`) from when it arrives.
  - A toast equal to one still showing (same level and text) does not stack: it merges into it, counts `×N` and starts its time again, like `push_toast`. A `Store` repeats a warning, such as one per line of a stream that does not parse (Store › Lines are read leniently). A failed connection attempt is not a toast: its error is the connection bar's second line, so a machine that is down shows none.
  - A tap dismisses it: there is no hover to show a close button on. A button fills the whole card, at least 44 px tall; it sits inside the `Glass` and not around it, since a button holds only phrasing content and `Glass` is a `div`.
- **Each one is a `Glass` card** with gpui's 3 px bar, `--accent` for info and `--failed` for error, with its glow. The stack lets touches through between the cards, so it never blocks the screen under it, and it is a polite live region.
- **Checked on 2026-11-09** in the iPhone 17 simulator, iOS 27.2: three equal error toasts and a fourth, pushed by a temporary line in `App.svelte` through a hot reload (nothing in the app sends a toast on demand), showed as one `×3` card and the other, under the two-line bar of `retrying`. A tap on the first removed it and the second moved up.

## Forms
