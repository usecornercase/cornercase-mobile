# Fit to this phone

A topic of [Terminal](../terminal.md): the switch that claims the tab with the phone's size, the machine taking it back, refusals, the background and being seen.

Code: `crates/core/src/terminal/fit.rs` (the claim and following it), `crates/core/src/terminal/live.rs` (`Terminal::run`, `Source`, `Unfitted`), `src-tauri/src/commands/terminal.rs` (`Shape`, `unfitted`), `src/lib/terminal/fit.svelte.ts`, `src/lib/terminal/opening.ts`, `src/routes/terminal/Terminal.svelte`.

- **The switch**: a button at the right of the terminal's bar, 44 px like `Back`, drawn with Lucide's `smartphone`, named `Fit to this screen` (`Fit to this phone` until 062, which brought tablets) and marked with `aria-pressed`, in `--accent` while on. It is on whenever the screen opens (Terminal › Fitted by default), also after it was turned off on the same tab, and a tap turns it off, back to the passive `surface`, for as long as the screen shows. Rejected: remembering that it was turned off, in memory per machine, `boot` and tab as the zoom is (043's P2): not asked for, and simple to add if it is missed.
- **On a tablet the same switch fits the pane** (062, `design/look/wide.md` › The terminal): in two panes the terminal's box is the detail, so the claim takes the columns that fit beside the board; `Full width`, a second switch before this one, only in a wide window, hides the side and the box grows, which opens a new view as a rotation does (A change opens again). Nothing here changed for it.
- **Opening the view is the claim, ending its process lets go**: on, `Terminal::run` starts `view --tab T --cols C --rows R`, active, sized and showing T, so it claims the tab as it opens, and once its first line shows T, `surface --view ID --patches`. Turning it off, leaving the screen and the background are a `terminal_open` with `fit: false` or a `terminal_close`, and `Active` aborts the task before, which drops both processes; cornercase drops the view of a process that ended and the tab goes back to the latest claim left, the machine's window. No `view-set`: the task asked for `view-set --claim` to claim again, but a new `view` claims as it opens, so nothing would call it. Rejected: keeping the view open and switching it with `view-set --claim`, `--inactive` or `--active` (an inactive view beside the passive `surface` was rejected in Terminal › Passive with the switch off; a `view-set` goes through the queue, which drops the command running when it reconnects; a process that ends lets go even when the app is killed, once the server's silence timeout ends it).
- **Nothing shows before the claim**: no frame is drawn until the view's first line shows T; a frame must name the view and the tab (`Source::View`), and each line's `projection` reaches the grid (`saw_projection`) before what follows it is drawn, so a frame made before the resize never shows (Terminal › The grid › `projection`).
- **The machine takes it back**: a line with `controls: false` while the view is still active, sized and on T means a later claim won, the machine's window used again or another view (gpui, another phone). The fit ends with `Screen::Fit(TakenBack)`, which becomes `unfitted` in `terminal-changed` and an `info` toast, `The machine took this tab back`; the task goes on with the passive `surface --tab` and the WebView turns the switch off. One text for both: the machine's window is the usual case, and the text is still true of a window on the machine. Not done: telling another view apart by the tab's `controller`.
- **Refused**: before its first line, a `view` that fails (a status other than 0, with cornercase's own text, such as `view N cannot size a tab now: …` or `no tab T`), that ends with status 0, or a line that is not a view, ends the fit with `Screen::Fit(Refused)`: `unfitted`, an error toast with that text, the switch off and the passive `surface`. While the `Store` has moved on (another transport, not connected), it tells nothing and opens the view again, as the `Store`'s state already says it.
- **An end is not a refusal**: once the first line came, any end of the `view` (status 0, a failure, the transport lost), and before it a lost transport or a stale boot (status 4, a restart), say nothing of the tab: the fit ends as a `surface` does (Terminal › The grid › Telling a failure: a failure or a loss with the `Store` still on its transport told once, status 0 never) and the tab is claimed again after a second, with the switch still on. A restart ends the `view` and the `Store`'s `status` together, in no fixed order, and the `Store` keeps its transport through it, so taking the `view`'s end for a refusal turned the fit off whenever the `view` was first. Rejected: telling those ends apart by the `Store`'s state alone, which that race defeats.
- **Moved away**: a line whose tab is not T (the tab closed and the view settled on a neighbour) draws nothing more and waits, up to 5 s, for the `Store`'s session to drop T, then goes back to the check of Opening it again, where a session without the tab ends the screen with `Closed` (`This tab was closed on the machine`), with no toast. The view's line can come before the `Store`'s `status` (paced at 500 ms) drops T, and a claim made in between is refused (`no tab T`), which turned the switch off with an error toast before the screen said the tab closed. After 5 s with T still listed, it claims the tab again. Rejected: taking the first refusal after a move as quiet, which leaves the race to the clock. A reconnection of the `Store` opens a new view on the new transport, still fitted.
- **The background by `visibilitychange`**: `Fit` holds the switch (`on`) and whether the page shows (`visible`, from `document.visibilityState`), and asks the screen's `Opening` (`ask`): on and hidden is a pause (`Opening.pause`, a `terminal_close` queued after any open on its way), so nothing runs for a screen nobody sees, as the task's CA3 asks; coming back opens it fitted again, and claims. With the switch off the passive `surface` stays open while hidden, as before the switch existed. Rejected: the passive `surface` while hidden with the switch on, a process on the machine and its traffic until iOS suspends the app. Rejected: Tauri's `WindowEvent::Suspended`, which also comes when Control Centre is pulled down without leaving the app and is not followed by a `Resumed`, so the phone would never claim again.
- **A change opens again**: `Opening` keys its calls on the size and the fit, so turning the switch on or off with the same size opens again, and a new size while fitted (a rotation, a physical keyboard's bar) opens a new view: the tab goes back to the machine's size for a moment, then takes the new one. The size is measured without the on-screen keyboard (Terminal › Typing › The height with the keyboard), so the keyboard opens nothing again. Left for later: `view-set --view ID --cols C --rows R` through the queue, with no bounce. After `unfitted` the WebView asks again with `fit: false`, which replaces the passive task Rust already runs with a new one: the `surface` restarts once, and `Active` keeps one task.
- **Seen while fitted**: an active, sized and visible view counts as seen, so while the switch is on the machine's `✓` clears and that agent's notices stop, where the passive `surface` never counts (Upstream gaps). With the switch off, an agent in `done` still loses its `✓` through `mark --reviewed`, but its notices go on (Chat › Seen from the phone). Cost: each line of the `view` stream is a whole session, as `watch`'s, at most two a second with its 500 ms pacing, while the switch is on.
- **The background, checked live** (2026-11-10, as in Fitted by default › Checked live fitted, the app sent to the background by opening Settings): `cornercase status` shows the tab's `controller` while the switch is on and none once it is off or the screen left. In the background with the switch on, the `controller` went 8 to 11 s after leaving, with the `view` and its `surface` ended and the `Store`'s streams kept, and coming back claimed the tab again within a second; with it off, the passive `surface` stayed until the bridge's silence timeout ended it, about 40 s later. So the simulator does suspend the app, not at once, and the close comes seconds late, why is not known; until then the machine's tab keeps the phone's size. To check on an iPhone: the real background, where iOS may suspend the app sooner with its sockets half open, and if the close does not reach the machine first, the tab keeps the phone's size until the bridge's silence timeout (45 s).
