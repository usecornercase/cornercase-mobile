# Terminal

Follows cornercase-gpui's `design/session-model.md` for the `view` and `surface` JSON and for applying frames and patches.

Topics, in `design/terminal/`:

- [The grid](terminal/grid.md) (`crates/core/src/cli/surface.rs`, `crates/core/src/cli/view.rs`, `crates/core/src/terminal/`): frames and patches applied in Rust by gpui's rules and where it differs, `projection`, the stream and opening it again, telling a failure, the terminal in the app, colours and styles, the pane in the WebView drawn in the DOM, zoom, opening it, a second passive reader, one pane on the phone, what goes to the WebView, the fixtures and the speed.
- [Fit to this phone](terminal/fit.md) (`crates/core/src/terminal/fit.rs`, `crates/core/src/terminal/live.rs`, `src-tauri/src/commands/terminal.rs`, `src/lib/terminal/fit.svelte.ts`, `opening.ts`, `src/routes/terminal/Terminal.svelte`): the switch, the view's opening as the claim and its end letting go, nothing before the claim, the machine taking it back, refused, an end that is not a refusal, moved away, the background by `visibilitychange`, a change opening again, seen while fitted and the background checked live.
- [Typing](terminal/typing.md) (`crates/core/src/cli/input.rs`, `src-tauri/src/commands/terminal/input.rs`, `src/lib/terminal/`, `src/routes/terminal/Terminal.svelte`): the contract's events, the pane's modes, the hidden `textarea` and its focus, the row of keys, a sticky Ctrl, paste, the height with the keyboard and Android's, Android keyboards, the accessory bar, swiping and swiping a fitted grid, what was checked live on iOS and Android and what is left to check on a phone.

- **Fitted by default** (043): every opening of the screen claims the tab with the phone's size (Fit to this phone), so the PTY takes the phone's columns and rows and the agent redraws at a width the phone can read. Found by the user on an iPhone (029, 2026-11-10): at the machine's width a tab cannot be read without zooming and panning sideways, and fitting it is what is wanted almost every time, which until then took a tap on every opening. The cost: opening the screen resizes the tab in the machine's window, and leaving it gives the size back; the tab counts as seen while the phone shows it, so the machine's `✓` clears and that agent's notices stop (Fit to this phone › Seen while fitted); a tab cornercase will not let the phone size is refused on every opening, each time with its toast; and nothing is drawn for the few milliseconds until the `view`'s first line shows the tab (Fit to this phone › Nothing shows before the claim). Rejected: passive by default, as from 020 to 043 (a tap on every opening to read at all); claiming with no way back (the agent redraws at the phone's width and the machine shows it cropped while the phone watches), so the switch stays; never claiming (no way to read at the phone's width).
- **Passive with the switch off**: the phone shows a tab with `surface --tab T --cols C --rows R --patches` alone, with no `view`, so the PTY keeps the width of the machine's window and the machine is not resized; the phone shows it with zoom and scrolling. Upstream, a view that is active, sized and showing a tab claims it as it opens (`views.md`, Who sizes a tab), so the `view` with the phone's size and without a claim that task 020 first asked for does not exist: an active one resizes the machine, an inactive or sizeless one draws nothing and refuses `surface --view`. Rejected: an inactive `view` kept beside the `surface` for the switch to turn on later (a stream nothing reads until then). The cost: a passive `surface` never counts as seen, so an agent's notices go on while the phone watches, and only a `done` agent's `✓` goes, by `mark --reviewed` (Upstream gaps, and Chat › Seen from the phone). The chat's question is still read this way (The grid › A second passive reader).
- **Checked live** (2026-11-09, iPhone Air simulator, iOS 27.2, cornercase 0.13.19 through the bridge, passive, as the screen opened before 043): with the screen open on an agent's tab (a stand-in named `claude`, not Claude Code working), the PTY stayed at the window's 87×40, nothing redrew at the phone's width, and the server ran one `surface` and no `view`, opened once and ended on going back. Checked again the same day once the board had taken Home's place (015), through the board, the agent's screen and its `Terminal` button, with a stand-in `claude` turning working and done every few seconds: the screen stayed open and live across those changes of the rows, on one `surface` process all along; the PTY kept its 127×48; `Back` went to the agent's screen and ended the `surface`, and a second `Back` to the board. With the screen open, `kill-server` then `start-server` made it open `surface --tab` again on the new boot, where cornercase had restored the tab with the same id (a new shell, as shells do not survive), and the agent's screen then said the agent no longer runs, with no `Terminal` button.
- **Checked live fitted** (2026-11-10, iPhone Air simulator, iOS 27.2, Tauri 2.12.5 in `ios dev`, cornercase 0.13.19 through the bridge, a `zsh` named `claude` with 500 lines of history, through the board, the agent's screen and `Terminal`): opening the screen ran a `view` and then its `surface`, with no `surface --tab` before; `cornercase status` gave the tab a `controller`, and `stty size` in the pane said 39 rows and 52 columns, the phone's, where the Mac's window had it at 48×139; the switch showed on (`aria-pressed`, in `--accent`). A tap turned it off: no `controller`, the PTY back at 48×139, one `surface`; another tap claimed it again with a new view. A `cornercase view --tab T --cols 80 --rows 24` started on the Mac took the tab: the toast `The Mac took this tab back` and the switch off. A new opening after the switch was turned off, from `Back` or the edge swipe, opened on and claimed.
- **"Fit to this phone"** (task 022; labelled `Fit to this screen` since 062, which also brought `Full width` beside it in a wide window, `design/look/wide.md` › The terminal) is the one way the phone sizes a tab: a switch, on at every opening, that claims the tab with the phone's size while it is on (Fit to this phone). In cornercase, the latest claim sizes the tab and the window takes it back when it is used.
