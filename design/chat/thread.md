# The thread

A topic of [Chat](../chat.md): what it holds and the gap line, your bubble, the live line and its no news, one live region, a message that grows, where the thread lands.

- **What it holds** (`src/routes/agent/Thread.svelte`, the bubbles from `threadOf` in `src/lib/thread.ts`): the pane's conversation as the phone saw it, in the order it saw it (`Chat.conversation`, Chat › Kept on the phone), then the live line. Your sends are bubbles on the right, the agent's messages markdown with when each was written, and what the phone could not see a line, `Earlier messages are on the machine`, in `--muted`. The order is the list's: the phone's and the machine's clocks are never compared. A read whose `text` or `written` differs from the agent's last message kept is a new message; one that grows it (the same `written`, or a text that starts with the kept one) or only ends its turn replaces it in place (`heard` in `src/lib/history.ts`). Asked by the user on 2026-11-10 ("it should look like a chat, not only the last message"); until then the thread held one message of the agent and your last send, and a new send replaced the bubble.
  - Sending while the agent was writing, its message grows after your send and is replaced in place, above your bubble, rather than coming under it as an answer. Accepted: it is one message of the agent, and a new message starting with the old text would be taken for it growing too.
  - **The gap line** goes before a new message of the agent the phone cannot vouch for, and at the start of every conversation kept on the phone, since what came before its first read is on the machine. The phone vouches from a message it read and from a send of this `Chat` that cornercase confirmed; opening the chat, coming back to the foreground (`Chat.follow`), a `pane-status-changed` with `pane: null` (Chat › Reading the message), the agent turning `working` or `shell` with no send of this `Chat` on its way (a prompt from the machine or another client; a send stops being on its way at the next change of status it sees, so one made into a running turn, which changes nothing, stops counting once that turn ends; only a change between `working` or `shell` and any other status counts, so a send made while `done` still counts after a `done` to `idle`, as the `mark --reviewed` the chat sends itself) and another agent in the pane (`row.agent`) all lose that (`Chat.lose`), so the next new message gets the line. `waiting` to `working` loses nothing: that is the agent going on after an answer, which the phone may have given with the keys or the question's buttons, and a line there would split every question from its answer. Nor does `working` to `shell` or back: `shell` is a turn over with a command still running in the background (Claude Code's `run_in_background`), and `shell` to `working` is the agent waking up by itself when it ends, the same work followed on screen. The limit: a prompt from the machine while `shell` gets no line. A send confirmed vouches for what follows it, which keeps an answer followed on screen without a line. Never two lines in a row nor one at the end: an `Edit` that would leave one takes it away too. Rejected: telling a gap by comparing `written` with the phone's clock.
  - **Between two reads**: while the agent works the phone reads every 10 s (Chat › Reading the message), so a text it wrote and replaced in between is never seen and leaves no line. Accepted: the phone cannot tell it, and cornercase's own history would close it (Upstream gaps).
- **Your bubble** (`src/routes/agent/Sent.svelte`) holds the text and, as chips, the names of the images that went with it; an image alone shows only its chips. Under it, its state: `Sending…` with the spinner, `Sent` with `check`, or `Not sent: <reason>` with `circle-alert` and, while it is live, `Retry` and `Edit` (Chat › Answering › What comes back). Its name for VoiceOver is `Your message, <state>`.
  - **Only the last send is live**: the last of your bubbles, while the `Chat` that sent it holds it (`Chat.outgoing`), keeps `Retry` and `Edit`; earlier ones, and every one loaded from the phone, show their state without buttons: their `Chat` and its images are gone, and `Edit` would bring back the text of another visit. A send still `sending` when its `Chat` went (the app killed, `Back` before the answer) shows `Not confirmed: the chat closed before an answer came back` with `circle-alert` (the chat, not the app: after `Back` the app is still open), its name for VoiceOver `Your message, not confirmed`: no answer will come for it, and it may have reached the agent, so it offers no `Retry` that could send it twice. How it looks is in Look › Components.
- **The live line** (`src/routes/agent/AgentLine.svelte`, `agentLine`) ends the thread and comes from `chat.row.status` (`agents-changed`), with no reads of its own:
  - `working`: the orbiting `StatusDot`, `<agent> is working…` and the time since `row.since` in the board's format (Board › Since when);
  - `shell`: the same, with `<agent> is waiting for a background command…`;
  - `waiting`: `<agent> is waiting for your answer`, and the composer keeps its note;
  - `failed`: `<agent> stopped with an error`, in the failure's tone;
  - `done`, `pending`, idle or a status it does not know: no line.

  Without `since`, which is the usual case on a phone after the background or a reconnection, the line has no time.
- **No news**: after `STALL_MS` (120 s) in `working` without news, the line turns `<agent> is working… · no news for N min · Ctrl-C interrupts`, in `--muted`. News is a status change, a read with another text or a confirmed send, timed on the phone's clock from the last one this `Chat` saw, or from when it opened (`Chat.lastNews`), never from `since`, which is often unknown. No alarm colour, haptic nor toast: a long turn is normal, and the next news brings the line back. The clock is the `Chat`'s injected `Clock`, so vitest moves it. It can be told only because the message is read while the agent works (Chat › Reading the message › When it is read again): without those reads every long turn would say it after 2 min.
- **One live region**: a single `aria-live="polite"` node in the line holds the send's state (`Sending`, `Sent`, `Not sent: <reason>`, hidden from the eye, since the bubble shows it) and the agent's phrase. The time runs outside it, and with no news so do its minutes and `Ctrl-C interrupts`, so VoiceOver says each change of phrase and not each tick. It stays in the page while the line has nothing to show, so `Sent` is still said to an agent that is done. Rejected: a region for the bubble and one for the line, which could speak at once.
- **A message that grows** is replaced in place: a read never goes back to `Reading its last message…`, so the text grows rather than flashing and the height does not jump. A message with `turn_over === false` ends with `More to come…` in `--faint`, only the agent's last one: an earlier one was followed by others.
- **Where the thread lands** (`landingAfter`, `sentAnew`, `lastAnswer` and `startTop` in `src/lib/thread.ts`): before each read or bubble that changes the thread, once a message shows, the screen checks whether the page, or the thread's own box while the keyboard is open (Chat › The screen › With the keyboard open), is at its end, within 48 px (`atEnd`), then once drawn:
  - **On opening**: the end, once the conversation kept draws, even when the first thing drawn is a long message just read.
  - **After your send or `Retry`** (a new `Chat.outgoing` in `sending`): the end, even when you were reading higher up: you just acted at the composer, and your bubble is where the answer will come. `Edit` and the bubble turning `Sent` or `Not sent` are not sends. Asked in issue #86 (from the TODO list, 2026-11-25); until then a send followed only from the end.
  - **An agent's message, new or grown, as the thread's last entry, with the thread at its end**: its start just under the sticky bar, or under the box's top with the keyboard open, which `under` (`src/lib/reveal.ts`) picks from the bar's rect, so the safe area, the connection bar and a wide window's `topmost` bar are all measured as drawn; never past the end and never back up. A long answer reads from its first line, with its time above it, and a short one shows whole with the thread at its end. Before #86 it followed to the end, which left a long answer at its last line. `NavBar` hands out its element (`bar`, bindable) through `ChatHeader` and `Agent`. When your bubble is the last entry (the agent's message grew above it), the rule is the end.
  - **Any other change at the end** (the reading note, your bubble's state): the end. **Not at the end**: it stays where it is, so reading higher up is never taken away, except by your own send.
  - Accepted: a message that grows while you read lower in it leaves you there, the new text coming under, rather than going back to its start or on to its last line.
  - Rejected: `scrollIntoView({ block: 'start' })` with `scroll-margin-top`, which needs the bar's height in CSS (it depends on the safe area, `--below` and `topmost`), scrolls the ancestors too and cannot say never back up; finding the bar from `Thread` by its class, which would tie `Thread` to `NavBar`'s markup; going to the start of every arriving message even when reading higher up, which takes reading away; smooth scrolling, since every other scroll of the thread is instant.

## Checked live

- **The thread, on 2026-11-09** in the iPhone Air simulator (iOS 27.2, Xcode 27.2, macOS 26.6.2, Tauri 2.12.5), against a bridge of its own on `127.0.0.1` and cornercase 0.13.19, with Claude Code 2.1.321 (Haiku 5.5), the taps made by XCUITest. A text sent from the field showed at once as your bubble under the agent's message, `Sending…`, then `Sent` within a second, with `claude is working… · now` under it and the header working; the agent's previous message, read while it worked, ended in `More to come…`. Its answer, about 900 words, came 13 s later under the bubble, whole, and the page, which was at its end, followed it to the end. Higher up, the order was your bubble, then the answer, with no live line once done. Each answer came in one read: none grew on the screen. Haiku answered every prompt within 30 s, so the line's time never passed `now`, and no news never showed.
- **Not sent and `Retry`**: with that bridge stopped, a text sent showed `Not sent: cannot reach the bridge: Connection refused (os error 61)` in its bubble, with `Retry` and `Edit`, while the connection bar counted down to its next try; the hidden live region held the same words. Once the bridge was back and the phone on it, `Retry` turned the bubble `Sent`, and the agent's answer showed under it.
- **The header**: down a long answer, the bar held `Back`, the agent's name with its dot and status, and `Terminal`, on the glass under the connection bar; back at the top the large header showed and the line went. With the keyboard open, before 041's review, WKWebView lifted the page so the field stayed above the keyboard, and both bars went out of sight with it (Chat › The keyboard); now both stay (Chat › The screen › With the keyboard open). As the keyboard closed after a send, the line showed for less than a second over the large header, until the page scrolled back.
